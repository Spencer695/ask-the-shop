# Changing or canceling an order

## Canceling an order
You can cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it with the Cancel order link in your confirmation email. After 1 hour your order may already be packed, so the fastest option is to return the items once they arrive.

## Changing items or sizes
We can't add or swap items on an order that has been placed. Cancel within 1 hour and reorder, or exchange the item after it arrives.

## Fixing your shipping address
You can update your shipping address any time before the order ships by replying to your confirmation email with the correct address. Once the order has shipped, we can't redirect it.
