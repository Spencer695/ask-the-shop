# Returns

## Return window and condition
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags attached. We can't accept items that smell of smoke, perfume, or pets.

## Final sale items
Sticker packs, enamel pins, gift cards, and anything marked Final Sale on the product page can't be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged.

## How to start a return
Go to the returns portal at fernwoodsupply.example/returns and enter your order number and email. Choose Refund or Exchange for each item. A prepaid return label costs $5.95, which we take out of your refund. Return labels are free when you choose an exchange.
