# Tracking your order

## Finding your tracking number
We email your tracking number as soon as your shipping label is created. You can also find it under Order history if you have an account. Check your spam folder if you can't find the shipping confirmation email.

## Tracking hasn't updated
It can take up to 24 hours after you get the shipping email for the carrier to scan your package, so tracking may show no movement at first. This is normal.

## Marked as delivered but not received
Sometimes a carrier marks a package as delivered a little early. Check with neighbors, your building's mailroom, and around your door. If it still hasn't shown up after 48 hours, email us with your order number. We will open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or a refund.
