# Gift cards

## Buying a gift card
Gift cards are digital only and come in amounts from $25 to $200. We email the gift card right away, or on a date you choose at checkout. We don't sell physical gift cards.

## Using a gift card
Enter the gift card code at checkout. You can use a gift card together with a discount code, and any balance left over stays on the card for next time.

## Gift card rules
Gift cards never expire and can't be returned or exchanged for cash. If the recipient lost the email, contact us and we will resend it.
