# Restocks, waitlists, and preorders

## Sold out sizes and restocks
Core items like the Classic Logo Tee and Heavyweight Hoodie usually restock within 4 to 6 weeks. Click Notify me on the product page to get an email when your size is back. Limited drops are marked Limited and won't restock.

## Preorders
Preorder items show an estimated ship date on the product page. You are charged when you place the preorder. If the date changes, we email you and you can cancel for a full refund.

## Orders with preorder and in stock items
If your order mixes preorder and in stock items, the whole order ships together once the preorder item is ready. Email us if you'd rather split it; we will ship the in stock items first at no extra cost.
