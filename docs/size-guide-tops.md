# Size guide: tees, hoodies, and crewnecks

## Classic Logo Tee
The tee is a unisex regular fit in sizes XS to 3XL. Chest width measured flat: XS 17 in, S 18 in, M 20 in, L 22 in, XL 24 in, 2XL 26 in, 3XL 28 in. It is 100% cotton and pre-shrunk, so it shrinks less than 3% in the wash. If you are between sizes, size up.

## Heavyweight Hoodie
The hoodie is made of 14 oz cotton fleece with a boxy, oversized fit. Choose your usual size for a relaxed look, or size down for a regular fit. Sizes run XS to 3XL.

## Crewneck Sweatshirt
The crewneck fits true to size and is made of 80% cotton and 20% polyester, so it keeps its shape after washing. Sizes run S to 2XL.

## How to measure
Lay a shirt you like flat and measure straight across the chest from armpit to armpit. Compare that number with the chest widths above.
