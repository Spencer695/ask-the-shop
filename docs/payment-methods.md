# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay.

## Paying in installments
Shop Pay Installments lets you split orders of $50 or more into 4 interest free payments, paid every two weeks. Choose Shop Pay at checkout to see if you qualify.

## When you're charged and sales tax
Your card is charged when you place your order. We collect sales tax on US orders based on your shipping address. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor, and we never see or store your full card number.
