# Bulk and wholesale orders

## Bulk orders for teams and groups
Orders of 24 or more pieces of the same item get 10% off. Email us with the item, sizes, and quantities for a quote. Bulk orders ship in about 2 weeks.

## Custom designs
We don't offer custom printing or embroidery. Bulk orders use our existing designs only.

## Selling our products in your store
We work with independent shops. Fill out the wholesale application on our website. The minimum opening order is $300, and reorders have a $150 minimum.
