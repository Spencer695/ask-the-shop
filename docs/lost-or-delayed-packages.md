# Lost or delayed packages

## When a package counts as lost
If your tracking shows no movement for 7 business days on a US order, or 15 business days on an international order, contact us with your order number.

## What we do about it
We contact the carrier on your behalf. Once the carrier confirms the package is lost, we reship your order at no cost, or refund it if the items are sold out. You do not need to file anything with the carrier yourself.

## Weather and holiday delays
During major storms and from Thanksgiving through New Year's, carriers can run several days behind. These delays are outside our control, but we are happy to check on your order.
