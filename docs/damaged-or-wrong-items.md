# Damaged, defective, or wrong items

## Reporting a problem
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days of delivery. Include your order number and a photo of the item and the packing slip.

## How we fix it
We send a free replacement or give you a full refund, including the original shipping cost. In most cases you don't need to send the item back.

## Problems that show up later
If a seam comes apart or a print peels within 90 days of normal wear and washing as described in our care guide, contact us and we will replace the item.
