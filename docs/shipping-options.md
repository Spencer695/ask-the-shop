# Shipping options and delivery times

## Shipping rates in the US
Standard shipping is $6.95 per order. Orders of $75 or more ship free with standard shipping. The $75 is counted after discounts and before tax. Expedited shipping costs $14.95 and is never free.

## Processing time
Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed after 1 pm Pacific time, or on a weekend or holiday, start processing the next business day. Preorder items follow the date on their product page instead.

## Delivery times
Standard shipping uses USPS Ground Advantage and arrives in 3 to 7 business days after your order ships. Expedited shipping uses UPS and arrives in 2 to 3 business days after your order ships.

## PO boxes and military addresses
We ship to PO boxes and APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses with standard shipping only. Expedited shipping is not available for these addresses.
