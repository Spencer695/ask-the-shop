# Washing and care

## Tees, hoodies, and crewnecks
Turn printed items inside out and machine wash cold with similar colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Don't iron directly on the print, and don't use bleach. Following these steps keeps prints from cracking and fading.

## Hats
Don't put the dad hat in the washing machine or dryer. Hand wash it in cool water with a little mild soap and let it air dry on a towel or a bowl so it keeps its shape.

## Totes
Spot clean the tote with a damp cloth. If it needs a full wash, wash it cold by itself and air dry it, since canvas can shrink a little.
