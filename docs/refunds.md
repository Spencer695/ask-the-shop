# Refunds

## Refund timing
We process your refund within 3 business days of receiving your return. After that, your bank or card issuer usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show the money in your account.

## Where the money goes
Refunds go back to your original payment method. If you paid with a gift card, the refund goes back onto the gift card.

## What is refunded
You get back the amount you actually paid for the item, after any discount code. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless we made a mistake with your order.
