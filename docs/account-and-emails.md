# Your account and email preferences

## Do I need an account?
No. You can check out as a guest. An account lets you see your order history and track orders in one place.

## Resetting your password
Click Sign in, then Forgot password, and enter your email. The reset link expires after 24 hours, so request a new one if it stops working.

## Email and text preferences
Every marketing email has an Unsubscribe link at the bottom. To stop text messages, reply STOP. You will still get order and shipping emails after unsubscribing.

## Deleting your account
Email us from the address on your account to ask for deletion. We delete your account and personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep for taxes.
