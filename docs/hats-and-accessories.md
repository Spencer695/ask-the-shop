# Hats, totes, stickers, and pins

## Embroidered Dad Hat
The dad hat is one size with an adjustable strap and brass buckle. It fits heads from 21.5 to 23.5 inches around. It is unstructured cotton twill with an embroidered logo.

## Canvas Tote
The tote measures 15 by 16 inches with 12 oz cotton canvas and 24 inch handles. It fits a laptop up to 15 inches.

## Sticker Pack
Each pack has 5 die cut vinyl stickers. They are waterproof and work well on water bottles, laptops, and cars. Hand wash bottles with stickers on them; dishwashers can lift the edges.

## Enamel Pin Set
The set has 3 hard enamel pins, each about 1 inch wide, with two rubber backs per pin.
