# Contact us

## How to reach us
Email help@fernwoodsupply.example or use the chat button on our website. Include your order number if your question is about an order.

## Support hours and response time
Our team answers Monday through Friday, 9 am to 5 pm Pacific time. We reply within 1 business day.

## Phone support
We don't offer phone support. Email and chat let us look up your order and send photos or links while we help.
