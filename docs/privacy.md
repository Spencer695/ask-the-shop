# Privacy and your data

## What we collect and why
We collect your name, email, shipping address, and order details to fulfill orders and answer support questions. If you opt in, we also use your email or phone number for marketing.

## We don't sell your data
We never sell your personal information. We share only what our shipping carriers, payment processor, and email provider need to do their jobs.

## Payment information
Our payment processor handles your card details. We never see or store your full card number.

## Your choices
You can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.
