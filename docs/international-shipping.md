# International shipping

## Where we ship
Outside the US we ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and Japan. If your country is not on this list, email us and we will tell you whether we can make an exception.

## International rates and delivery times
Shipping to Canada is a flat $18. Shipping to every other international destination is a flat $24. Free shipping offers do not apply to international orders. International orders usually arrive in 7 to 14 business days.

## Duties, customs, and import taxes
Duties and import taxes are not included at checkout. Your country's customs office or the delivery carrier collects them from you on delivery. If you refuse to pay them and the package comes back to us, we refund your order minus the original shipping cost.
