# Local pickup and pop-up events

## Free local pickup
Choose Local pickup at checkout to pick up your order for free at our studio in Anaheim, California. We email you when it's ready, usually within 1 business day. Bring your order number.

## Studio pickup hours
Pickup hours are Tuesday through Saturday, 11 am to 5 pm. The studio is for pickups only, not a retail store.

## Pop-up markets
We sell at pop-up markets around Orange County. Event dates are posted on our Instagram. We take cards and phone payments at events. Items bought at an event can be returned by mail within 30 days with your receipt.
